Ordering at your table

You don't sign up or give your name. Your phone gets one cookie that links it to your table's order and is deleted when you close the browser. Orders are kept for the venue's accounts but aren't linked to you.

Booking

We ask for your name and phone number, and optionally your email and any special requests, only to hold and manage your booking. We need these to fulfil your booking (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Staff see them, and we never sell them or use them for marketing. Your booking, with all its details, is deleted automatically twelve months after your visit.

Notifications

If you allow order alerts, your browser's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla, depending on your browser) delivers them. Your subscription is deleted after two days.

Cookies

We only use cookies the site needs to work: the table cookie above and a staff login cookie. There is no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking, and fonts and scripts are served from this site.
