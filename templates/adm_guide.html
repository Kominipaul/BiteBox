How it works

Everything you need to run the venue with this app, from the first order to the advanced tools.

Start here: setting up a new venue

  1. Change the default password you got with the software. You're asked to on the first login.
  2. Settings: your venue name, payment methods (cash on or off) and your business details for receipts.
  3. Categories: create the sections guests browse (Coffee, Salads, Cocktails…). For each one, choose if the kitchen or the bar makes it. That decides who receives the order.
  4. Menu & stock: add your items with price, photo and description.
  5. Map editor: place your tables and give each one a zone (Terrace, Beach, Inside…). Print each table's QR code.
  6. Staff: create an account for each person and pick their role.
  7. Menus & hours: set when the kitchen and bar open. That's all most venues ever need there.
  8. Test it: scan a table's QR with your own phone, order something, and follow it through the staff app.

Start here: your shift as a manager

  1. Sign in. You land in the staff app (Work). The Work / Admin switch at the top takes you to the back office and back.
  2. Orders shows every order from every station. Collect lists tables that still owe money.
  3. Floor shows who is sitting where and who called for help. Bookings shows today's arrivals.
  4. Something runs out? More → Sold out, tap the item. Kitchen swamped? Mark it Busy or close it for 30 minutes.
  5. A staff member forgot their password? Admin → Staff help lets you set a new one.

Start here: kitchen and bar

  1. Sign in on the station tablet or your phone. You see the Queue: only the items your station makes.
  2. A new order chimes. Tap Start preparing when you begin, then Mark ready when it's done. The waiter gets it straight away.
  3. To make at the top adds up everything still open (e.g. 4× Freddo), so you can batch.
  4. Ran out of something? Sold out tab, tap the item. Guests stop seeing it at once.
  5. On a mounted tablet: open the menu (your initials) and switch on Keep screen on.

Start here: waiters

  1. Sign in on your phone and switch on Phone alerts in the menu (your initials). You get a buzz even with the screen off.
  2. Deliver shows orders the kitchen or bar marked ready. Take them to the table and tap Delivered.
  3. A guest pressed Call waiter? It shows at the top with the reason (bill, water, help). Tap On my way.
  4. Collect lists tables that still owe cash. Take the money, tap Mark paid.
  5. Floor and Bookings: seat arriving guests, free tables when people leave.

Start here: DJ

  1. Sign in. You see Requests: songs guests asked for from their table, with the tip they left.
  2. Tap Play it to accept or Skip to decline. The guest sees your answer on their phone.
  3. The owner can switch song requests on or off in Settings.
The whole thing in one minuteHow an order travels
  1. Guest scans the QR code on the table. The menu opens in their phone's browser, with no app to install.
  2. They order and choose how to pay. The order goes straight to the staff app.
  3. Kitchen gets the food, bar gets the drinks. A mixed order is split automatically.
  4. The station taps Start preparing, then Mark ready.
  5. The waiter is alerted, delivers it and taps Delivered.
  6. Cash is collected at the table (Collect → Mark paid).

Every screen updates live: you never need to refresh. The guest follows the progress on their phone (Received → Preparing → Ready → Delivered).

Signing in and your phonePassword, alerts, home screen
  • Sign in at /login with the username and password the owner gave you. A new account may ask you to pick your own password first.
  • Change your password any time: menu (your initials) → Change password, or Password in the sidebar.
  • Install it like an app: in your phone's browser, use Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen with the venue's icon. On iPhone this is required for phone alerts.
  • Sound alerts: a chime for new and ready orders. The bell at the top turns it on or off.
  • Phone alerts: notifications on the lock screen. Allow notifications when the phone asks.
  • Keep screen on: for a tablet mounted in the kitchen or at the bar.
  • The Live dot at the top shows the connection. If it drops, a bar says Connection lost and it reconnects by itself.
  • Finished your shift on a shared device? Sign out.
Roles: who sees whatOwner, manager, waiter, kitchen, bar, DJ
Owner (admin)Everything, including settings, staff accounts, look & brand, backups and the sales export.
ManagerEvery station in the staff app, plus Dashboard, Bookings, Floor, Menu, Orders, Logs and Staff help. No settings, accounts or branding.
WaiterDeliver (ready orders), Collect (payments), help calls, Floor and Bookings.
Kitchen / BarThe queue of their own items and the Sold out list. They can close or mark busy their own station.
DJSong requests only.

Need something in between, e.g. a waiter who may edit the menu? In Staff, open the account, tick Custom under Page access and choose the pages. Settings, accounts, branding and the order feed always stay with the owner.

Working the queueKitchen and bar, step by step
  • Each card is one order: the table, the order #, how long ago it came in, and the items.
  • no onion (red) means remove it, + extra cheese (blue) means add it. A Note is the guest's own words. Read it.
  • + 2 more items made elsewhere means the other station is making the rest of the order.
  • Start preparing → Mark ready. Pressed it by mistake? Tap Undo in the message at the bottom.
  • Print ticket prints a kitchen ticket without prices.
  • Cancel order only works before the order is ready. It puts the stock back. Talk to the guest or a manager first.
Sold out, close early, busyWhen the night doesn't go to plan
  • Sold out tab: tap an item to hide it from guests at once. Tap again when it's back. Items with a stock count turn sold out by themselves when they hit 0.
  • Close for 30 min, 1 hour, today or until reopened: guests can't order anything from that station. Drinks keep selling if only the kitchen is closed.
  • Busy +15 / +30 / +45 min: you stay open, but guests are told to expect a longer wait. It switches itself off after an hour.
  • Kitchen staff can switch the kitchen, bar staff the bar, and managers both. Owners do the same on Menus & hours.
Delivering and help callsReady orders, Call waiter
  • Deliver lists everything marked ready, oldest first. Deliver, then tap Delivered. The guest's phone updates.
  • Call waiter from a table shows at the top of every waiter's and manager's screen, with the reason: Wants the bill, Wants water / napkins / cutlery or Needs help. Tap On my way so colleagues know it's handled.
  • How fast calls were answered is kept in Logs.
Payments, tips and refundsCash, card, splitting the bill
  • The guest chooses cash or card when ordering. The owner can turn cash off (card only) in Settings.
  • Cash: the order shows Cash · unpaid · €. Collect groups unpaid orders by table. Take the money, tap Mark paid. Wrong table? Undo.
  • Card orders are marked paid as soon as they're placed.
  • Tip and Split N ways show on the order so you know before you reach the table.
  • A guest can cancel an order that hasn't started. If it was already paid, it becomes Refund requested. A manager settles it and fixes the order in Orders.
Floor and bookingsSeating, walk-ins, freeing tables
  • Floor is the live map: free, reserved, seated, and tables where a guest is ordering by QR or needs help. Tap a spot for details.
  • New booking (the + button): pick Phone booking when they call ahead, or Walk-in when they're standing there (seats them right now).
  • When booked guests arrive: Seat them. When they leave: Finish · free spot.
  • Didn't show? Mark No-show. Running late shows as late with the minutes.
  • A QR table that left without closing: Release table (guest left). Tables nobody used for 45 minutes free up by themselves.
  • Broken or closed spot: Out of service. It can't be booked or ordered from.
What guests can doSo you can answer their questions
  • Browse the menu by category, search, and see photos and prices.
  • Customize an item: remove ingredients or add paid extras.
  • Leave a note for the kitchen, add a tip, and say if they're splitting the bill.
  • Follow the order live, get an alert when it's ready, and open the receipt.
  • Call waiter with a reason, cancel an order before it's started, and request a song (if switched on).
  • Book a table online at /book (if switched on). No payment is taken online.
  • One phone orders per table: the first to scan becomes the host. Others see hosted by another guest. The host can tap Leave Table, or a waiter can release the table, to hand it over.
Menu and stockItems, prices, photos, extras
  • Menu & stock: tap an item to change its name, price, description, photo (camera, gallery or a link) and stock.
  • Stock: a number counts down with every order and turns sold out at 0. Unlimited for things you don't count.
  • Customisation: Included, can remove (onion, sauce) and Extra add-on with a price (extra cheese €1). Guests then see Customize.
  • Serving hours → Only between: e.g. fresh juice only until 12:00.
  • Categories: what guests browse by. The kitchen/bar choice routes the order, so a wrong choice sends the item to the wrong screen.
  • Changes show on every guest's phone immediately.
Opening hours and special daysMenus & hours, the simple part
  • Menus & hours → Opening hours: tap Set hours on the kitchen or bar. Choose Same every day or Different by day, and Last orders (e.g. 15 min before closing).
  • Without hours, it's Open all day: guests can always order.
  • Special days → Add: a date or a range that's closed (holiday, private event) or has different hours (winter season). A note tells guests why.
  • Outside opening hours, guests can still browse the menu but can't order.
Areas: different rules for each part of the venuePause an area, give it its own hours

Areas are the zones you give tables in the Map editor (Beach, Terrace, Rooftop, VIP…). With two or more, Menus & hours shows an Areas list.

  • 1 h / Today / Pause: stop orders from that area only, e.g. the beach when it's windy. Everyone else keeps ordering.
  • + Own hours: create a menu just for that area: its own days, times and dishes (e.g. Beach 10:00–19:00, drinks and salads only).
  • Each area lists the menus and offers that apply only to it, so you see its rules at a glance.

One area or none? The list stays hidden. Kitchen and bar hours cover the whole venue.

Custom menusBrunch, late night, a menu per area

For anything opening hours can't do. A custom menu has days, a time window, last orders, the areas it applies to, and what it serves: everything, chosen categories or single dishes.

  • Weekend brunch Sat–Sun 10:00–13:00, only the Brunch category.
  • Late night Fri–Sat 23:00–03:00, bar plus crêpes. A menu that ends before it starts runs past midnight.
  • Rooftop: drinks only, in the Rooftop area.

Important: once a switched-on menu covers an area, tables there can only order what a running menu serves. Areas no menu mentions keep ordering everything. Each menu has an on/off switch, so you can keep seasonal menus and switch them on when needed.

How the rules combineWhy an item is or isn't orderable

For a dish to be orderable at a table, all of these must allow it, in this order:

  1. Its station isn't closed by hand.
  2. The table's area isn't paused.
  3. No special day closes it, and the station is within its opening hours (minus last orders).
  4. The dish's own serving hours, if it has any.
  5. If custom menus cover that area: a running menu serves it.
  6. It's not sold out.

Not sure? Use Check what guests see on Menus & hours: pick an area, a day and a time to see exactly what's orderable. The Check your set-up box warns you about rules that don't fit together (e.g. a menu running when the kitchen is closed).

Happy hour and offersLower prices at set times
  • Menus & hours → Happy hour & offers → Add offer: days, times, % or € off, areas, and what it covers.
  • While it runs, guests see the old price crossed out and how long the offer lasts.
  • If two offers cover the same item, the lower price wins. The price never goes below €0.
Guest suggestionsRecommended, Popular, best sellers first
  • Recommended: your own picks at the top of the menu, under your heading (e.g. Chef's picks).
  • Popular: what sells most at this time of day, from the last 7, 30 or 90 days. Only items being served right now are shown.
  • Best sellers first: sorts categories and items by sales for the time of day, so drinks rise at night. It's a monthly snapshot, not reshuffled after every order. Re-rank now refreshes it.
  • Show dishes coming later: greys out items that open later (From 19:00) instead of hiding them.
Staff accounts and securityCreate, reset, lost phone, leaving staff
  • Staff → New: username, password (6+ characters), role, and optional custom page access. Tell the person their password in person.
  • Forgot password: open the account, set a new one, tell them. They're signed out everywhere. Managers can do this from Staff help.
  • Lost or stolen phone: Sign out everywhere on that account.
  • Someone leaves: Deactivate. They're thrown out immediately, and their history stays. Activate again if they come back.
  • History on each account shows what was changed, and by whom.
  • Give each person their own account. Shared logins make the logs useless.
Reports and your dataDashboard, orders, logs, export, backup
  • Dashboard: revenue, orders, bookings, guests expected, free spots and items running low, live.
  • Orders: every order, searchable by number, table, item or staff. Tap one to see items, payment and receipt, or to Fix this order (mark paid or unpaid, change the status, cancel).
  • Logs: call-waiter response times and every staff-account change.
  • Export CSV (owner): the orders matching your filters, for your accountant or spreadsheet.
  • Backup (owner, on the Dashboard): downloads a full copy of everything: menu, orders and accounts. Do it regularly and keep it somewhere safe.
Map editor and QR tablesSpots, zones, booking rules
  • Drag spots to move them. Tap one to edit its label, zone, min / max guests, minimum spend, booking length and online hours.
  • Changes save straight away and show on the guest page.
  • The zone is the area used by Menus & hours, so name zones the way you'd talk about them (Beach, Terrace).
  • Print each table's QR code from Map editor → QR codes and stick it where guests see it. Each code holds a secret link, so nobody can order for a table without scanning it. Lost or copied sticker? Press New code on that table and reprint it.
Settings, booking and brandMake it yours
  • Settings → Venue: the name guests see. Business details: VAT number and tax office on receipts.
  • DJ requests and Payment methods (cash on or off): one switch each.
  • Online booking: on or off, first and last arrival time, default length, how many days ahead, and a phone number for guests who'd rather call.
  • Look & brand: start from a preset, then adjust colours, logos, fonts, the login page and the welcome text. A live preview shows it, and nothing changes for anyone until you save.
Good to know before going liveLimits of this version
  • Card payments aren't connected to a payment provider yet: choosing card marks the order paid without charging anyone. Take card payments on your own terminal.
  • Receipts are for the guest's information. They aren't sent to the tax authority and don't replace your legal fiscal receipts.
  • Stations are kitchen and bar. A venue with only one sees only that one.
Something's wrong: quick fixesCommon problems
A guest says an item is missing or greyed out
Check Sold out, then the station's hours and close switch. A manager can use Check what guests see for that area and time.
No orders are arriving
Look at the Live dot. If it's off, check the Wi-Fi. Then check that the station isn't closed and the item's category belongs to your station.
No sound or phone alerts
Turn on Sound alerts and Phone alerts in your menu and allow notifications. On iPhone, add the app to the Home Screen first. Keep the phone off silent.
I forgot my password, or lost the phone with my login codes
Ask a manager (Staff help) or the owner to reset it. The password they give you works once: you pick your own straight after, and two-step login starts over.
The owner forgot the admin password or lost their phone
Whoever runs the server can reset it with go run ./cmd/resetpassword admin. It prints a one-time password; the next login asks for a new password and sets up two-step login again.
What is two-step login?
After your password, the login asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone, so a stolen password alone isn't enough. Admin accounts must use it; anyone else can turn it on from Two-step login in their menu.
A table shows occupied but nobody's there
On Floor, Release table (guest left). Otherwise it frees itself after 45 minutes of no activity.
Marked the wrong order
Tap Undo straight away, or fix it later in Orders → Fix this order.
